<p>NicerTooltip adds decorations to the game tooltip. Custom backgrounds for different types of units / objects.<br>It also allows you to stay near your cusor instead of the Edit Mode position (optional).<br><br>It's still in dev! As I'm leveling through Forever, I'm trying new things for this addon. If you have any idea, feel free to discuss it in the comments!</p>
<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
<li>An ornate frame around your tooltips.</li>
<li>A themed background that matches what you're looking at: players, creatures, items, spells, gathering nodes and more.</li>
<li>Background opacity and brightness you can adjust, and themed art that can be resized or hidden.</li>
<li>A portrait medallion on players and creatures, showing their portrait or class icon (or nothing at all).</li>
<li>Optionally show the level of players and creatures next to their name.</li>
<li>Optionally show a player's realm, its language and type, and the realms connected to it.</li>
<li>A Compact mode for a smaller, tidier tooltip.</li>
<li>A minimum tooltip width, so tooltips don't change size all the time.</li>
<li>Tooltips can follow your mouse cursor instead of staying in the corner of the screen.</li>
<li>Choose what happens to tooltips in combat: hide them, keep them in their usual place, or keep them on your cursor.</li>
<li>Works on Retail, all the Classic versions and WoW Forever.</li>
<li>Everything is set in Options &gt; AddOns &gt; Nicer Tooltip, or by typing <code>/ntt</code>.</li>
</ul>
